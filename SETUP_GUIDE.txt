----------------------------------
STEP BY STEP SETUP
----------------------------------

STEP 1 - Register Company
We (system provider) will log in as super admin and register your company.
We will create your head office account and share the login details with you.

You will receive:
- Company ID (example: acme)
- Username
- Password


STEP 2 - Head Office Login
Open the app.
Enter Company ID, Username, and Password.
Tap Sign In.


STEP 3 - Create a Branch
Go to Branches.
Tap Add Branch.
Fill in branch name and code.
Tap Save.


STEP 4 - Create a Staff Account
Go to Users.
Tap Add User.
Fill in name, username, and password.
Select the branch.
Tap Save.

Share the Company ID, username, and password with the staff member.


STEP 5 - Staff Login (First Time)
Staff opens the app.
Enters Company ID, username, and password.
Taps Sign In.

Staff will see a "Pending Approval" screen.
This is normal. Their device needs approval before they can use the system.


STEP 6 - Approve the Device (Head Office)
Go to Device Approvals.
Find the pending request.
Tap Approve.

The staff app will automatically update and they will be logged in.

To reject - tap Reject.
The staff member will not be able to log in from that device.


STEP 7 - Done
After approval, staff can log in normally and start using the system.


NOTE: Every new device needs approval.
If a staff member changes their phone, they will go through the approval step again.
